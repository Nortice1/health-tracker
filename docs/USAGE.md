# Daily use

Open your private `/exec` app URL in a browser signed in to your Google account.

| Area | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Train | Choose date/exercise, enter weight and reps, optionally effort/notes, then save. Check the displayed unit. Use undo for the newest mistaken set. |
| Stats | Review trends, training days, weekly muscle volume and lift history. Empty charts need logged data. |
| Body | Enter weight, body fat or daily nutrition fields for the selected date and save. A date is updated rather than creating separate copies. |
| Coach | Ask a question or describe a meal. Inspect each proposed change before tapping Save; cancel incorrect proposals. Food photos are estimates. |
| Saved foods | Save a known meal as a favorite, then use its logging button for the selected date. Verify portion and nutrition first. |
| Cardio | Use Log cardio for manual entries or import a TCX export you already have. Verify parsed date, units and activity before saving. |
| Interval timer | Follow the on-screen timer and enter measured max HR if known. Set your own age/resting HR in the source before relying on default zones. |

The spreadsheet is also usable directly. Read its Guide tab. Enter values only in input columns and keep formulas/helper columns intact. The exercise table controls exercise type, muscle, rep range, weight increment and target sets. Bodyweight exercises use added weight plus body weight for load; check the exercise notes.

Coach examples:

```text
Meal: rice, grilled chicken and vegetables. Estimate a medium plate.
Log 3 sets of Incline Bench Press: 40 kg for 10, 9 and 8 reps today.
How has my training changed over the last four weeks?
Cardio: walked 30 minutes, 2 km today.
```

For saved changes, verify the corresponding Log, Body, Meals or Cardio row. Review calorie and protein totals after meals. Do not log the same meal both as a favorite and through Coach. Imported cardio and manually logged sessions can duplicate each other too.

Settings values and the starter exercise plan are examples, not recommendations for every user. Choose your own goals. Back up your Sheet before bulk edits or updates. API/model errors affect Coach; the ordinary training/body logging functions do not require a Gemini key.
